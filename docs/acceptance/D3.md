# D3 acceptance evidence

## Automated evidence

The D3 lifecycle tests cover:

- canonical resolve preview without creating a Follow;
- invalid, missing, and unavailable provider errors mapped to safe product errors;
- a first follow ensuring no more than 10 videos through the injected artifact provider;
- no summary-provider call during resolve or initial backfill;
- global Channel reuse and fresh-frontier reuse for a second user;
- unfollow isolation and Undo/refollow;
- server-side follow-limit rejection with no discovery/backfill calls;
- bounded partial backfill that keeps the Follow and schedules a retry state;
- the service-role-only, idempotent follow-limit RPC through the D1 PGlite migration tests.

Checks run on the D3 branch:

- `npm test`: PASS (all tests);
- `npm run typecheck`: PASS;
- `npm run lint`: PASS;
- Node 24 Docker production build: PASS.

## Live provider evidence

Blocked in this environment. `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`, the Media Monitor sidecar URL, and the three explicitly configured sidecar paths are absent. No live channel resolve, RSS discovery, or sidecar video ensure was attempted, and no deployment was performed.

## Deliberate exclusions

D3 does not implement populated Feed behavior or pagination, automatic summary generation or charging, background synchronization, Scheduler/internal sync routes, billing, deployment, WebSub, queues, or later DAG nodes. Follow backfill is metadata-only and bounded to at most 10 provider ensures.
